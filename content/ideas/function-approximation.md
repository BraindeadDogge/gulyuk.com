This essay grew out of [Mikhail Romanov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mikhail-v-romanov/)'s machine learning basics lecture in the JetBrains Foundation's Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence program, and much of its framing and math owes to him. To practise the ideas hands-on, try his course [Mathematics for Deep Learning](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/33838-deep-learning-pt-1-mathematics) in the JetBrains IDE plugin catalog.

This is an interactive essay. Every figure is playable, and after the first one a bar appears that lets you change the target function all the figures fit. Equations sit in boxes marked "skippable": nothing essential lives only there.

Soqra is the AI-powered maths platform I build as its full-stack engineer. In May 2026 we brought it to Aspire Private British School for a second visit, this time with 10th graders: a lecture on using AI responsibly, then practice on the platform. In the survey, 93% rated the experience 4 or 5, and 87% believe AI can genuinely help their learning. Their main request was more school material inside the platform, which we are already working on.
